# Juvly Aesthetics brand extraction

System: warm ivory clinical-luxury canvas, soft muted-rose surfaces, deep charcoal CTAs, restrained champagne-taupe details, image-first proof, and generous whitespace.

## Tokens

- `--bg`: `oklch(98% 0.007 68)`
- `--surface`: `oklch(100% 0 0)`
- `--fg`: `oklch(25% 0.004 50)`
- `--muted`: `oklch(47% 0.01 55)`
- `--border`: `oklch(88% 0.017 42)`
- `--accent`: `oklch(76% 0.045 18)`

## Type

- Display: `Cormorant Garamond`, `Georgia`, serif
- Body: `Montserrat`, `Avenir Next`, `Helvetica Neue`, sans-serif
- Mono: `ui-monospace`, `SFMono-Regular`, monospace

## Layout posture

- Use a side-by-side hero so face-critical imagery stays intact and text never overlays faces.
- Favor thin borders, quiet rose-tinted panels, and deep-charcoal buttons over heavy shadows.
- Preserve proof images with natural proportions; use `object-fit: contain` for before/after and face-critical assets.
- Keep the accent budget restrained: blush for atmosphere, champagne for small metadata/rules, charcoal for conversion CTAs.
- Let the sales-page structure lead the rhythm; imagery supports each section rather than becoming decorative wallpaper.
